# Privacy Operations Portfolio

**Practical HIPAA, 42 CFR Part 2, and privacy program work for behavioral health platforms**

This portfolio demonstrates hands-on privacy operations capabilities relevant to roles involving healthcare privacy, individual rights, incident response, vendor governance, and product/AI privacy assessments.

The three projects below were built as focused, realistic artifacts. Each maps to core operational responsibilities: maintaining controls and data maps, managing rights requests and incidents to statutory deadlines, and conducting consistent assessments of products, AI use cases, and vendors.

---

## Projects

### 1. HIPAA / 42 CFR Part 2 Privacy Operations Playbook & Controls Register
Practical controls register, data map / records of processing, operational metrics, and short playbooks for a behavioral health environment.

**Key outcomes demonstrated:**  
Current controls with owners and evidence requirements · Data mapping · Testing schedules · Metrics tracking · Actionable playbooks
